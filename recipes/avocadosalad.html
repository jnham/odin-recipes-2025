Avocado Salad

Description

A delicious combination of creamy avocados, sweet and juicy tomatoes, bell pepper, cilantro, and fresh limejuice for an easy summer salad full of bold, fresh flavor and color.

Ingredients

  • Avocados: 2 peeled, pitted, and diced.
  • Onion: 1 chopped sweet onion.
  • Bell pepper: 1 chopped green bell pepper.
  • Tomato: 1 large chopped ripe tomato.
  • Cilantro: 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro.
  • Lime: 1/2 lime, juiced.
  • Seasonings: salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Combine avocados, onion, bell pepper, tomato, cilantro, and lime juice in a large bowl.
  3. Gently toss until evenly coated. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Enjoy!
